關於 Clouding MediaAbout Us

我們相信，好的品牌會自己說話。 We believe great brands speak for themselves.

Clouding Media 由一群來自媒體、廣告與科技產業的夥伴組成。我們把多年在前線累積的洞察,化為陪伴品牌成長的策略與作品。 Clouding Media brings together professionals from media, advertising, and tech. We turn years of frontline insight into strategies and work that help brands grow.

我們的故事Our Story

從一個簡單的相信開始It started with a simple belief

十多年前,我們在大型媒體與代理商裡見證了無數品牌的起落。我們發現：真正能成功的品牌,不是預算最多的,而是說故事最真誠的。 Over a decade in big agencies and media houses, we watched countless brands rise and fall. The pattern was clear — the brands that succeeded weren't the ones with the biggest budgets, but the ones who told their story most honestly.

於是 Clouding Media 誕生了。我們想做一個更貼近品牌、更敢直白給建議、更願意陪客戶走長路的團隊。 That's why Clouding Media exists — a team built to stay close to brands, give honest advice, and walk the long road together.
